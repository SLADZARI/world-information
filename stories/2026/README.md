# 2026 research stories

Submit each investigation as `YYYY-MM-DD-short-slug.md` using `templates/story.md`.

## Suggested first investigation
**Centralized exchange security incident → market liquidity and confidence.**

Treat the reported security event as a research question pending validation. Collect exchange announcements, on-chain records (with chain and block identifiers), exchange withdrawal status, BTC/ETH/BNB prices with exact exchange/time/quote currency, derivatives liquidations with methodology, and independent reporting. Compare against global risk appetite, macro releases, and other market events. Do not assume that a coincident market move was caused by the incident.

Possible hypotheses to test: (H1) exchange-specific counterparty stress, (H2) general risk-off coinciding with the incident, (H3) forced liquidations amplifying an initial price move. Resolve each against explicitly timestamped evidence.
