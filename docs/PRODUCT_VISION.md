# World Information — Country & Global Systems Observatory

Status: product proposal v0.1 (2026-10-30). Independent public project. **Dementor Tab** is an existing game, **not** the name of this product.

## North-star interaction
A person selects a country and an **as-of date**, sees a comprehensible country environment, chooses a lens (living / relocating / investing / researching), drills into a specific change, opens a causal investigation, compares alternative scenarios and evidence, then subscribes to updates or contributes a counterargument.

**Not a country score or news feed.** It is an inspectable, multilingual, contributor-driven system of signals, impacts, and falsifiable explanations.

## Three views built from one canonical dataset
1. **Country environment**: thematic indicators, major changes, comparable baselines, conditions for different residents, recent events, linked foreign exposure. Never collapse multi-dimensional conditions into one unsupported "country health" score.
2. **Cause and consequence board**: interactive directed multigraph scoped to a country/event, toggleable domains and epistemic layers, inspectable edges with sources, competing mechanisms, loops over time.
3. **World map + time machine**: geographic shocks, trade and energy flows, country-to-country edges, time window/as-of comparisons, saved scenario branches and forecast track records.

## Research objects are not interchangeable
A news mention is a signal; an event is an occurrence; a measured outcome is an indicator change; a cause is a defended claim; an opinion is a discussion post; a market price is a traded price; a scenario is conditional reasoning. Do not convert one to another automatically.

## Audience-specific views, same evidence
- Investor: exposure pathways (currency, capital mobility, regulation, political risk, supply chains); no personal financial recommendation.
- Relocator: residency conditions, healthcare, housing, wages, rights by legal status, service access, data update date; no country-wide score pretending equal rights for all residents.
- Local resident: services, prices, environmental conditions, elections/policies, alternative explanations; differentiate national from city-level indicators.
- Journalist/researcher: provenance, competing narratives, time-series methodology, revisions, citation exports.
- Forecaster/contributor: resolved questions, model assumptions, debates, predictions with outcomes; no gambling execution.

## Success tests (proposed, not proven)
- Can a first-time visitor find one decision-relevant indicator and its source in 60 seconds?
- Can they distinguish confirmed observations vs competing hypotheses?
- Can they click any relationship and see why it exists, including contrary evidence?
- Can a participant submit a correction via GitHub without learning database internals?
- Can a forecast be scored without rewriting the original claim?
